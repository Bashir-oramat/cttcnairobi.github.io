Nairobi's Top Table Tennis Club

Based at City Stadium, at the beginning of Jogoo Road.

City Table Tennis Club (CTTC) is the club where talent is nurtured, from a child's first rally to a national champion's podium. Our certified coaches deliver structured training across four programmes.

Affiliated with Table Tennis Kenya (TTK), we compete at local, regional, and national levels throughout the year.

Training Hours

  • Mon – Fri: 4:00 PM – 8:00 PM
  • Saturday: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
  • Sunday: Closed – No Training

A Programme for Every Player

From your first rally to the national podium — all levels coached under one roof.

🧒 Beginner & Kids

Fun, safe introduction for children and adults. Learn grip, footwork, service, and basic rally techniques.

→ EVERY WEEKDAY

🏃 Recreational Adult

Social sessions, internal leagues, and round-robins for working adults seeking competitive but relaxed play.

→ EVENINGS & SATURDAYS

🏆 Competitive & National

High-performance coaching targeting county and national competition. Tactical coaching and multi-ball drilling.

→ DAILY SESSIONS

☀️ Holiday Camps

Intensive school-holiday camps with full-day sessions, mini-tournaments, and certificates.
